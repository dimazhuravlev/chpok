Privacy Policy — Chpok!

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Chpok! does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

No data collection

The app has no network functionality. It does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any third-party services. It does not require an account and does not create any user identifier.

Local storage

The app stores one item on your device: the state of your current unfinished game, including the board layout, score, remaining lives, and elapsed play time. This file never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Device access

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health data, or any other sensitive information. It uses haptic feedback only.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
